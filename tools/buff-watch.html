Getting timers in here

  1. In game: /log on (once per character).
  2. Target yourself or someone else and run /inspectbuffs. Broadcast it across your boxes to refresh everything at once.
  3. Hit Connect logs… and pick your EverQuest Logs folder. This page then tails every eqlog_*.txt and keeps counting down on its own.

The log is a snapshot, not a stream — so each line's timestamp gets added to its "remaining" value to get a real expiry, and the countdown runs from there. Re-run /inspectbuffs any time to resync.

Pop-out

Pop out opens a floating always-on-top window (Chrome/Edge). Browsers can't make a page background truly transparent, so this is an opaque mini-window rather than a see-through overlay. Run EQ in Windowed or Borderless Windowed or it'll paint over the top.

No folder picker?

Firefox and Safari don't support the folder API. Use Paste — dump log text straight in and it'll parse the same way.

Paste log text

Paste any chunk of log containing --You sense … remaining.-- lines. Timestamped lines are preferred; untimestamped lines are treated as "just now".
